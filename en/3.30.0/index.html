Path of Exile 3.30.0 · Patch Notes

Covens of Umbra

✦

Notes posted: November 10, 2026 · Launch: November 13, 2026

The essentials

  • New Covens of Umbra league: pick Fates for the area boss and use Viscera on your Wickerman.
  • Scarabs are removed and replaced by Threads, up to 8 per map.
  • The Atlas Passive Tree is fully reworked, now with 150 points (was 138).
  • Item Rarity now decides which drop pools can roll, so monsters with more Rarity drop better currency.
  • Atziri moved into the Temple of Atzoatl, and Sacrifice and Mortal Fragments are removed.

⚔League

  • Disrupting the coven's ritual lets you pick Fates for the area boss, raising both difficulty and rewards.KEY
  • Fate-empowered bosses drop Viscera, which you place on your own Wickerman to claim some of their power.
  • The league has 40 new challenges plus 8 in Ruthless, and from the 19th challenge you earn Totem Pole pieces.
  • Mirage is now core: it appears in maps and The Black Barya is on the Atlas in all leagues, at area level 84 (was 83).
  • Harvest no longer has its own subarea and now uses Sacred Flora spread through the map.
  • Gwennen no longer sells gamble items and now offers random crafts on Rare items for 1 Astragali.

🗺Atlas

  • Scarabs are removed and replaced by Threads for 10 mechanics; each Thread adds its mechanic to the map, up to 8 per map.KEY
  • Atlas Passive Tree reworked: you start at the centre, with 150 total points (was 138).KEY
  • Abyss, Breach, Legion, Ritual and the other primary mechanics no longer spawn randomly in maps; you need Threads.KEY
  • Shaped Regions reworked: there are 10 types, each granting free Threads to maps in the region.
  • Delirium Orbs now reforge Rare maps with 5 to 50% Delirious, up to 100%, plus 1 extra reward per 20%.

✦Skills

  • Winter Orb now fires 2 Projectiles at all levels (was 2-3) and deals 307 to 460 at level 20 (was 360 to 539).KEY
  • New skill Arcane Link: links you to an ally, who gains lightning damage based on your mana.
  • New transfigured Fireball of Radiation: applies a short, strong fire damage over time debuff.
  • Flame Link now grants 219 to 329 Added Fire at level 20 (was 169 to 254), but 3% of max Life (was 5%).
  • Vaal Sacrifice Support now sacrifices all but 20% of Life and Energy Shield (was all but 1).
  • Golem buffs were reduced: Stone Golem grants 16% Defences (was 20%).

♛Ascendancies

  • Reliquarian Notables have been shuffled for Covens of Umbra.
  • Pathfinder's Master Distiller: Bismuth penetrates 30% (was 25%) and Diamond grants +100% Critical Strike Multiplier.
  • Trickster's Heartstopper now grants 75% less Damage over Time (was 50%).
  • Warden's Unbound Avatar now lasts 15 seconds (was 10).

◆Items

  • Item Rarity now picks drop pools: more Rarity removes scrolls and common currency and favours valuable currency.KEY
  • From Act 6, Rare and Magic items can drop with an Item Tier from 2 to 5, giving better mods and more sockets.
  • Added 11 new Unique Items and the Mycelium Core Jewel base.
  • Shavronne's Wrappings lost 10% faster ES recharge start and gained 20-30% of Damage Taken Recouped as ES.
  • Doedre's Tongue only drops from Covens of Umbra encounters and now has 30-35% resistances (was 20-25%).
  • Mercenaries now get -30% resistances in level 46-67 areas and -60% in 68+ areas.

⚗Crafting & currency

  • Orb of Chance now gives Uniques much more often, but it is much rarer and no longer sold by vendors.KEY
  • Orbs of Binding can no longer be obtained, and Sacred Orbs are more common.
  • New bench option locks 1 prefix or suffix for 1 Divine Orb, or 2 for 2 Divine Orbs.
  • Modifier Tier Rating reworked: it no longer changes the chance of each mod type and has no breakpoints.
  • Vendoring 2 Rares with the same name gives a Chaos Orb, and 3 give an Exalted Orb.
  • Altar of Corruption and Lapidary Lens now corrupt an item a second time (Twice Corrupted).

☠Bosses & monsters

  • Atziri moved to the Temple of Atzoatl: with the Cradle of Creation, the temple is level 84 and has Uber Atziri.KEY
  • The Atziri fight can no longer be paused, and she uses empowered abilities below 66% life.
  • The Saresh, of the Weeping Black fight is significantly more difficult.
  • Rogue Exiles have much higher Rarity and grant more experience.

☰UI & QoL

  • Scrolls of Wisdom can now be bought in every town for 15 Gold.
  • Most "nearby" effects now state their exact range in metres.
  • The Fragment Stash Tab has a new Threads section.
  • New bench option makes all sockets White for an Orb of Scouring.
